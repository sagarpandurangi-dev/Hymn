# Handoff: Hymn & Pamphlet ID video promos

Paste this whole document into a new chat (Gemini, or any assistant) to carry on
the promo work without the app-building history.

## 1. What the products are

- **Hymn**: a personal life copilot app from India. It helps people plan anything,
  from "45 minutes of guitar this week" to "a house at 29, a wedding at 30, a child
  by 32". It checks those plans honestly against their real money and real free
  time, then keeps them on track. It covers five areas: money, health, family and
  relationships, learning and work.
- **Pamphlet ID**: a 7-character personal ID that people share with shops instead
  of their phone number. A normal ID looks like `S482915` and is free. Special ones
  cost money: name aliases like `PRIYA88`, birthdays like `S150890`, and lucky
  numbers like `S777777`. Each ID exists only once.
- **Owner's taste**: modern, energetic, not boring. Wants percussive music (tapping
  drums, percolating instruments), real chaos turning into calm, and a clear
  emotional release.

## 5. Rules for every promo

- **Honest claims only.** Never say "no more spam ever" or "blocks all calls"; say "you decide who can reach you". No promises of outcomes such as "get a house" or "get pregnant". No fake urgency ("limited time", countdowns). "Each ID exists only once" is true.
- **No real people's faces, no real brands or logos**, and don't name telecom companies, banks or regulators.
- **Royalty-free music only.** AI-generated audio may miss the beat, so use a licensed percussive / afro-house / tabla-style track and cut to it.
- **Captions on:** most people watch Reels without sound.

## 6. How to produce it (Gemini Veo)

1. Veo makes clips of about 8 seconds. Paste the brief, then one shot at a time.
2. Generate shot 1 until the lead actor looks right, then start every later shot with *"Same actor(s), same outfit and colours as before."*
3. If Gemini answers with text, switch it to its video (Veo) mode first.
4. Join the clips in CapCut (or Canva video). Add text, music and voiceover there; AI-drawn on-screen text often comes out garbled.
5. For crisp app screens, overlay real Hymn screenshots in the editor instead of AI-drawn screens.
6. Use a still from the strongest calm frame as the Reel cover.
